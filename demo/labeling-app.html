Open in SignaVis Lite

SignaVis Lite is this labeling app as a desktop program for Windows, macOS and Linux: projects in real folders, your own audio files reopen after a restart, works offline.

⬇ Download SignaVis Lite Free, open source. After installing, links can open in the app.

The installers are not code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS show a warning — how to install.

No Xeno-canto API Key configured

Without an API key, labels from Xeno-canto cannot be loaded. The recording will play, but annotations will be missing.

You can add your key at any time via XC API Key in the top bar (Upload tab). Get an API key at https://xeno-canto.org/account.

Recording too long

Support for long recordings is planned. For now, please use recordings under 10 minutes for best results.

Recording not found

This Xeno-Canto recording could not be downloaded.

Double-check the XC number. The recording may not exist, may have been removed, or may not have a publicly downloadable audio file.

Your recordings and labels

Audio files, spectrograms, detections and labels are processed locally in your browser. They are not uploaded to us. Settings and projects are stored in your browser's local storage or in a folder you choose, and you can delete them at any time.

Web analytics (Umami)

We use a self-hosted instance of Umami (stats.sommertheater-altrossthal.de) to collect anonymous usage statistics (page views, referrer, browser, operating system, device type, country). Umami sets no cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not track you across websites. The data stays on our server and is not shared with third parties. If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, no statistics are collected.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in understanding how the application is used and improving it). The desktop app does not use analytics.

Third-party services loaded on demand

Some features load content from external servers. Your browser then connects directly to these providers, which receive your IP address and technical request data. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing these features).

  • unpkg (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) and jsDelivr (Prospect One, Poland, delivered via Cloudflare/Fastly): program libraries (audio player, map, machine-learning runtime). Data may be transferred to the USA.
  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, UK): map tiles for recording locations. Privacy policy.
  • xeno-canto (Xeno-canto Foundation, Netherlands): search, download and upload of recordings when you use the xeno-canto features; your API key is sent only to xeno-canto.
  • GBIF (Global Biodiversity Information Facility, Denmark): species and reference data in the reference-call view.
  • CORS proxies (codetabs.com, corsproxy.io, allorigins.win, thingproxy.freeboard.io): used only as a fallback when a xeno-canto download is blocked by the browser; the requested file URL and your IP address pass through the proxy.
