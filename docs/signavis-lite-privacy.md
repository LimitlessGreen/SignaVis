SignaVis Lite is a non-commercial open-source desktop application ([source code](https://github.com/LimitlessGreen/SignaVis)).

## In short

- Your recordings, labels and projects stay on your computer. They are never uploaded to us.
- The app has **no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no account**.
- It connects to the internet only for the features listed below. Each of these servers sees your IP address and ordinary technical request data (time, requested file, app/browser version).

## Your recordings, labels and projects

Audio files, spectrograms, detections (including BirdNET analysis) and labels are processed locally on your computer. Projects are saved in a folder you choose or in the app's data folder; settings are stored in the app's local storage. You can delete them at any time — uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

## Internet connections

The app connects to these services, and only when you use the related feature:

| Service | When | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| **xeno-canto** (Xeno-canto Foundation, Netherlands) | searching, opening, downloading or uploading xeno-canto recordings | search terms, recording IDs; your xeno-canto API key and uploads only when you upload |
| **GitHub Pages / GitHub** (GitHub Inc., USA) | first use of BirdNET or species images (model and data files, then kept offline), *Help* links | file requests |
| **GitHub Releases** (GitHub Inc., USA) | update check once a day and *Check for Updates…* — **not** in the Microsoft Store version, which is updated by the Store | current version and platform |
| **GBIF** (Global Biodiversity Information Facility, Denmark) | the reference-call view | species names |
| **OpenStreetMap** (OpenStreetMap Foundation, UK) | showing a recording location on the map | map tile requests |
| **CORS proxies** (codetabs.com, corsproxy.io, allorigins.win, thingproxy.freeboard.io) | only as a fallback when a xeno-canto download fails directly | the requested file URL |

GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR); data may be transferred to the USA ([GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)). OpenStreetMap: [privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR — providing the features you use and keeping the app up to date.

## Microsoft Store

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes data for the download, licensing, updates and crash reports under its own [privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). We only receive aggregated, anonymous statistics from Partner Center (e.g. number of installs per country).

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15–20 GDPR), and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). Since we do not store personal data from the app, there is usually nothing on our side to access or delete. You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, e.g. the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner (Sächsischer Datenschutz- und Transparenzbeauftragter).
