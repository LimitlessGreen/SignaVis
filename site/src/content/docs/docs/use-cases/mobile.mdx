<img class="shot" src="/SignaVis/media/app-mobile.png" alt="SignaVis on a smartphone: spectrogram with detected species" style="max-width: 320px; margin-inline: auto;" />

<ol class="steps-m3">
  <li>Open SignaVis on the smartphone.</li>
  <li>Under **Recordings**, enter an XC number or URL and tap **Load**.</li>
  <li>Tap **Analyse with BirdNET**.</li>
</ol>

The detected species appear as cards below the player and as bars above the spectrogram.
