<p class="lede">This walkthrough takes about ten minutes. You need a bird recording (WAV, MP3, FLAC or OGG) and a current browser – nothing to install.</p>

:::tip[No recording at hand?]
Paste any [Xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org) number (for example `XC1059662`) into the **Xeno-canto number or URL** field on the **Project** tab and press **Load**.
:::

## 1. Open SignaVis and load a recording

Open the [web app](/SignaVis/demo/labeling-app.html). On first start a short setup asks for your name and licence – they are written into every annotation set you export. You can **Skip setup** and fill it in later on the **Upload** tab.

Drag the audio file into the window, or use **Audio file** on the **Project** tab. **Record** captures audio from the microphone instead.

<img class="shot" src="/SignaVis/media/getting-started-loaded.webp" alt="SignaVis with a loaded recording: waveform on top, spectrogram in the middle, overview strip below" />

The window has three parts:

- **Left rail** – the work areas: *Project*, *Labels*, *Analyse*, *Species*, *Upload*.
- **Centre** – waveform, spectrogram and, below it, the overview strip of the whole recording.
- **Right rail** – *Display* (colours, contrast, frequency range), *Properties* of the selected label, and *Audio* settings.

## 2. Read the spectrogram

Time runs from left to right, pitch from bottom (low) to top (high), and brightness shows loudness. A whistled note is a thin line, a harsh call a broad smear, and a trill a row of repeated short marks.

- **Space** plays and pauses; click the spectrogram to jump.
- **Mouse wheel** scrolls in time, **Ctrl + wheel** zooms in time, **⇧ + wheel** zooms in frequency.
- **Fit** shows the whole recording. Details: [Navigation & zoom](/SignaVis/docs/features/overview/).

## 3. Let BirdNET suggest species

Open **Analyse**, keep **Species · BirdNET** and the model **V3.0**, and press **▶ Analyze**. The model runs on your device; in the default *local* mode no audio is uploaded.

When the analysis is done, SignaVis switches to **Labels**. The suggestions appear grouped by species in the **BirdNET** source, and as coloured tracks below the spectrogram.

<img class="shot" src="/SignaVis/media/getting-started-analysed.webp" alt="Labels tab after the analysis: BirdNET found 14 detections of 6 species, Blue Jay with 8 detections at the top" />

## 4. Check the results

BirdNET makes suggestions, not decisions. Before you trust a species:

1. **Look at the count.** In the example, Blue Jay was found 8 times – plausible. Five other species appear only once each; treat those with suspicion.
2. **Listen to it.** Expand a species and click a detection: the view jumps there. Play it and compare the shape in the spectrogram with a reference recording.
3. **Raise the bar.** Under **Analyse → Settings**, a higher **Min. confidence** (default 0.25) removes weak suggestions.
4. **Filter by place and season.** Set a location and choose a [species list](/SignaVis/docs/use-cases/species-lists/) so that only species expected there are suggested.

Accept a detection you have verified with **✓** – it is copied into **My labels**. Hide the rest with the eye icon.

## 5. Annotate a call yourself

Hold **⇧** and drag a box around a call on the spectrogram. Type the species (common or scientific name, or a short code) and confirm with **Enter**. Drag the corners to fit the box tightly; keys **1–7** set the sound type (song, call, alarm …).

More in [Annotate calls](/SignaVis/docs/use-cases/label-calls/) and [Label boxes](/SignaVis/docs/features/labels/).

## 6. Save and export

Your work is kept automatically in the browser project (*Saved* on the **Project** tab). For longer projects choose **Use a project folder…** so the files live on your disk.

To get the labels out, open **Labels → Export**:

| Format | Use it for |
|---|---|
| Labels JSON | Archiving, reloading in SignaVis, own scripts |
| Raven table | Raven Pro and most analysis tools |
| Annota-JSON (XC) | Uploading annotation sets to Xeno-canto (**Upload** tab) |
