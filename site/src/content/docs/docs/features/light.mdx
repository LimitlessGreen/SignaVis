<ol class="steps-m3">
  <li>Open **Properties** in the right sidebar.</li>
  <li>Under **Recording**, check **Date**, time and **Location**. Values are taken from the file metadata where available and can be entered manually.</li>
  <li>The **Light** section shows the recording in the course of the day and of the year.</li>
</ol>

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Sun & twilight | Solar elevation over the day with night, twilight and day phases; the recording time is marked |
| Recording | Light phases the recording passes through, with their durations |
| Dawn / Dusk | Times of astronomical (sun at −18°), nautical (−12°) and civil (−6°) twilight and of sunrise and sunset |
| Season & day length | Day length over the year; the recording date is marked |

**Chart** and **Dial** switch between time lines and astronomical dials.

## Clock of the recording time

The zone menu sets the clock in which the recording time is given: local time (with summer time) or another time zone. File names from AudioMoth recorders are often in UTC. The choice is stored with the recording.

:::note
The calculation is accurate to a few minutes between the polar circles. Without a recording time, only the date is placed in the year.
:::
