<a href="/SignaVis/media/interface-tour.webp"><img class="shot" src="/SignaVis/media/interface-tour.webp" alt="SignaVis window with 19 numbered areas: top bar, work areas, side panel, playback and tool bars, waveform, spectrogram, zoom, overview, label tracks, right panels and status bar" /></a>

<ol class="tour-legend">
  <li>**Template** – Species and tags (**+ Tags**) given to every new label without the dialog. Empty the field to switch it off.</li>
  <li>**Label language** – Language of species names (e.g. *en_uk*, *de*, scientific).</li>
  <li>**XC API Key** – Your Xeno-canto key – needed to upload annotation sets and for some downloads.</li>
  <li>**Open in app** – Hands the current recording to the SignaVis Lite desktop app.</li>
  <li>**Theme & help** – Light/dark theme; **?** opens help, shortcuts and *About*.</li>
  <li>**Work areas** – **Project** (load recordings, project folder) · **Labels** · **Analyse** (BirdNET, BirdBox) · **Species** (species lists) · **Upload** (to Xeno-canto). The badge counts labels.</li>
  <li>**Side panel** – Content of the selected work area – here the labels, grouped by source (*My labels*, *BirdNET*) and species. ✓ adopts, the eye hides.</li>
  <li>**Transport** – Start · −5 s (**J**) · play/pause (**Space**) · stop · +5 s (**L**) · end.</li>
  <li>**Time** – Playhead position and recording length.</li>
  <li>**Volume & speed** – Mute; playback speed – slowing down also lowers the pitch, so ×10 makes bat calls audible.</li>
  <li>**View** – Follow playhead · loop · crosshair · **Fit** (whole recording) · reset zoom.</li>
  <li>**Tools** – Draw mode · stamp mode (**Ctrl + D**) · Snap to call · magnifier.</li>
  <li>**Waveform** – Loudness over time; labels appear as shaded spans.</li>
  <li>**Spectrogram** – Time left→right, pitch bottom→top, brightness = loudness. **⇧ + drag** draws a label; the bar at the top shows BirdNET detections.</li>
  <li>**Zoom** – Time and frequency zoom; **Auto** fits the frequency range to where the calls are.</li>
  <li>**Overview** – The whole recording; drag the window or its handles to move or resize the view.</li>
  <li>**Tracks / Table** – All labels as tracks along the timeline, or as an editable table.</li>
  <li>**Right panels** – **Display** (colours, contrast, frequency range) · **Properties** of the selected label · **Audio** settings.</li>
  <li>**Status bar** – Version, active species list, visible time range and *Report issues*.</li>
</ol>
